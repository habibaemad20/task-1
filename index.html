Chicken Pasta

Ingredients:

Pasta, chicken breast, cooking cream, garlic, butter, parmesan, salt, pepper

Steps:

1. Boil pasta in salted water

2. Cook chicken in butter + garlic

3. Add cream + cheese

4. Mix with pasta and serve

Cheese Burger

Ingredients:

Beef patty, burger bun, cheddar cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, sauce

Steps:

1. Season and grill beef patty

2. Toast bun lightly

3. Add cheese on hot patty

4. Assemble burger with sauce & veggies

Pancakes

Ingredients:

Flour, milk, eggs, sugar, baking powder, butter

Steps:

1. Mix all ingredients until smooth

2. Heat pan with butter

3. Pour batter and cook both sides

4. Serve with honey or syrup

Sweet and spicy

Ingredients:

Chicken, soy sauce, honey, chili sauce, garlic, rice

Steps:

1. Cook chicken cubes

2. Mix soy sauce + honey + chili

3. Glaze chicken with sauce

4. Serve over rice

Ratatouille

Ingredients:

Zucchini, eggplant, tomato, bell pepper, garlic, olive oil, herbs

Steps:

1. Slice all vegetables

2. Sauté garlic in olive oil

3. Cook vegetables slowly

4. Season and serve warm

Crepe

Ingredients:

Flour, milk, eggs, butter, sugar, pinch of salt

Steps:

1. Whisk all ingredients into smooth batter

2. Heat non-stick pan

3. Pour thin layer and cook both sides

4. Fill with chocolate or fruits
